`@runa-cmd/mcp` leaves beta at `0.2.0`, and requires `@runa-cmd/core` `^1.0.0`. It stays at `0.x` while the rest of Runa reaches 1.0, because MCP is where the API may still change: until its own 1.0, a minor version may change its contract, as its error `class` values and its codes, and its changelog says how to migrate. It reaches 1.0 after four weeks without such changes.
